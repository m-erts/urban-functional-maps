Test 3. Compare the map, not the count

One matrix, one published validity criterion, three maps. The criterion says when an area is acceptable. It does not say how to repair one that is not.

Source: ONS WU01EW; MSOA 2011 BGC; TTWA 2011 super-generalised boundaries. OGL v3.0.

The error matrix, counted in people

C[a, b] = employed residents who are in delimited area a and in official area b. Everything else is read from it.

IoU(a, b) = C[a,b] / (C[a,·] + C[·,b] − C[a,b])
omission(b) = 1 − C[a*,b] / C[·,b]
commission(b) = 1 − C[a*,b] / C[a*,·] a* is the delimited area with the highest IoU for official area b. Adjusted Rand index and V-measure come from the same table.

Where the boundaries diverge: six cases, one by one

Chosen by rule from the 2021 matrix, not by eye. The next six slides take them in turn.

Light amber: delimited areas. Darker: the area in question. Red: units in question. Black and grey lines: official TTWA 2011.

Case 1. Attachment without adjacency

  1. RuleA unit joins the area that receives the largest share of its outgoing commuters, if that share is 15 % or more. The rule looks at flows only.
  2. Result18 units with 34,324 employed residents join the Liverpool area. The nearest unit of that area is 1.1 km away, across water.
  3. Extent26 of 235 areas are in more than one piece; 79 units are cut off.
  4. ReadingThe polygons do not cross the river. A tunnel does. By flows the fragment belongs where the rule put it.
Source: ONS ODWP01EW; MSOA 2021 BGC. OGL v3.0. Red: the fragment. Darker amber: the area it joined.

Case 2. The repair

  1. RuleIn each area the piece that holds the core stays. Every other piece goes to the adjacent area with the largest two-way flow across the shared border. Adjacency is queen adjacency of the polygons.
  2. ResultThe fragment now belongs to the area around Wirral.
  3. CostSelf-containment moves from 0.695 to 0.696.
  4. ReadingThe repair gives a map that can be drawn. In this case it replaces a true functional link with a polygon rule. Both versions are in the tables.
Source: ONS ODWP01EW; MSOA 2021 BGC. OGL v3.0.

Case 3. An island the repair cannot place

  1. RuleA piece with no neighbour in another area has nowhere to go. It stays and is reported.
  2. Result9 units of Isle of Anglesey, 17,948 employed residents.
  3. Extent3 areas keep such a piece after the repair, 13 units in all.
  4. ReadingPolygons end at the shore; two bridges do not count as adjacency. The list of islands is an output of the pipeline, not a defect to hide.
Source: ONS ODWP01EW; MSOA 2021 BGC. OGL v3.0.

Case 4. An official area inside a larger one

  1. OfficialMedway, 166,407 employed residents (black line).
  2. MatchThe delimited area that starts at City of London (darker amber).
  3. Error matrixIoU 0.06. Omission 7 %: nearly all of the official area is inside the match. Commission 94 %: nearly all of the match is outside it.
  4. ReadingThe heuristic has no lower bound on self-containment of what it attaches. One strong core takes its whole commuter belt, other labour markets included.
Source: ONS ODWP01EW; MSOA 2021 BGC; TTWA 2011. OGL v3.0.

Case 5. An official area cut into several

  1. OfficialSlough and Heathrow, 401,486 employed residents (black line).
  2. Match5 delimited areas hold parts of it. The best one holds 44 % of its employed residents: omission 0.56.
  3. Error matrixIoU 0.44. Commission is 0: the match lies inside the official area.
  4. ReadingSeveral cores pass the inflow quantile here and do not exchange 10 % of their outflow, so they are not merged. The count of areas rises where employment is polycentric.
Source: ONS ODWP01EW; MSOA 2021 BGC; TTWA 2011. OGL v3.0.

Case 6. A unit the official boundary cuts

  1. CauseOfficial areas are built from blocks smaller than the units of the open matrix.
  2. ResultMonmouthshire 005: 41 % of its blocks lie in the official area it is assigned to (hatched unit, black line).
  3. Extent332 of 7,264 units are cut by an official boundary.
  4. ReadingNo partition of these units can reproduce the official map. Agreement values carry this error and cannot reach 1. This is the zoning problem at the level of the unit.
Source: ONS Open Geography, OA 2021 to TTWA 2011 best-fit look-up. OGL v3.0.

Four tests that need only the open file

  1. Readthe code list. Where do people without a trip land in the matrix?
  2. Shuffled + (1 − d) h, then random contiguous partitions.
  1. Comparethe map with a reference through the error matrix, in people.
  2. Testfor clusters before naming classes; state the thresholds.
Where sources agree the result holds. Where they differ, the difference is the finding.

Limits

  • One country with pairs. The decomposition is general; its magnitudes are shown for England and Wales only.
  • The 2021 census day was a lockdown day.
  • Official areas are built from smaller blocks; 332 units are cut.
  • The contiguous null is region growing, not a uniform sample. Its partitions have 35 effective areas against 24.
  • The official algorithm was not run; nor was the open European implementation.
  • The linear form of the validity trade-off is an assumption.
  • The trace test has 11 cells.
  • GHSL epoch 2030 is a projection: 6.87 million people against about 6.65 million in the census.
  • Who is in the Japanese panel is not published. Cells under 10 people are absent.
  • The Overture release used has left the public bucket. The aggregate is archived in the repository.
  • Spanish hourly operator matrices are not processed.

What keeps the numbers honest
One parameter file. One file of results. Documents rendered from templates: no number is typed.
A frozen snapshot is the regression target of the tests.
Reconciliation tests: sums equal published totals, joins are one to one, structural zeros are explained.
Input files listed with address, licence and SHA-256.
The run repeated in a fresh environment from the lock file gave the same values.
