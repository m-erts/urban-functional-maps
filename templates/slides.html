omission(b) = 1 − C[a*,b] / C[·,b]

  1. RuleA unit joins the area that receives the largest share of its outgoing commuters, if that share is 15 % or more. The rule looks at flows only.
  2. Result{{uk_case_attach_units}} units with {{uk_case_attach_workers:,.0f}} employed residents join the {{uk_case_attach_area}} area. The nearest unit of that area is {{uk_case_attach_distance_km:.1f}} km away, across water.

Source: ONS ODWP01EW; MSOA 2021 BGC. OGL v3.0. Red: the fragment. Darker amber: the area it joined.

Case 2. The repair

  1. RuleIn each area the piece that holds the core stays. Every other piece goes to the adjacent area with the largest two-way flow across the shared border. Adjacency is queen adjacency of the polygons.
  2. ResultThe fragment now belongs to the area around {{uk_case_attach_new_area}}.
  3. CostSelf-containment moves from {{uk_sc_2021_areas:.3f}} to {{uk_sc_contiguous_2021:.3f}}.
  4. ReadingThe repair gives a map that can be drawn. In this case it replaces a true functional link with a polygon rule. Both versions are in the tables.
Source: ONS ODWP01EW; MSOA 2021 BGC. OGL v3.0.

Four tests that need only the open file

  1. Readthe code list. Where do people without a trip land in the matrix?
  2. Shuffled + (1 − d) h, then random contiguous partitions.
  1. Comparethe map with a reference through the error matrix, in people.
  2. Testfor clusters before naming classes; state the thresholds.
Where sources agree the result holds. Where they differ, the difference is the finding.

Limits

  • One country with pairs. The decomposition is general; its magnitudes are shown for England and Wales only.
  • The 2021 census day was a lockdown day.
  • Official areas are built from smaller blocks; {{uk_msoa_cut_by_ttwa_2021}} units are cut.
  • The contiguous null is region growing, not a uniform sample. Its partitions have {{uk_own_contiguous_null_effective_areas_2021:.0f}} effective areas against {{uk_effective_areas_2021:.0f}}.
  • The official algorithm was not run; nor was the open European implementation.
  • The linear form of the validity trade-off is an assumption.
  • The trace test has {{osm_hij_three_way_n}} cells.
  • GHSL epoch 2030 is a projection: {{rs_pop_ghsl/1e6:.2f}} million people against about 6.65 million in the census.
  • Who is in the Japanese panel is not published. Cells under 10 people are absent.
  • The Overture release used has left the public bucket. The aggregate is archived in the repository.
  • Spanish hourly operator matrices are not processed.

One parameter file. One file of results. Documents rendered from templates: no number is typed.
A frozen snapshot is the regression target of the tests.
Reconciliation tests: sums equal published totals, joins are one to one, structural zeros are explained.
Input files listed with address, licence and SHA-256.
